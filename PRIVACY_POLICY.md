## 1. Overview
GarbaConnect is dedicated to connecting Garba dancers, enthusiasts, and Navratri celebration participants. We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding personal information collected through our mobile application. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, disclose, and secure your information in compliance with the **Google Play Developer Program Policies** and applicable data protection regulations.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Voluntarily Provide
- **Account Registration & Authentication:** Name, email address, password, age, gender, and city.
- **Profile Data:** Garba skill level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Pro), dance style interests (Traditional Garba, Raas Garba, Dandiya Raas, Teen Taali, etc.), partner preferences, profile bio, avatar selection, or user-uploaded profile photographs.
- **In-App Messaging & Swiping:** Swipe interactions (like, super like, pass), mutual match records, and end-to-end chat communication between matched users.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
- **Device & App Performance Data:** Crash logs, diagnostics, and app performance metrics collected via Google Play Services / Firebase Analytics to ensure uptime and resolve defects.
- **Network State:** Connectivity status to gracefully transition between online cloud matching and offline local state.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use your data solely to:
- Authenticate users securely and protect against bot spam or fraudulent accounts.
- Match dancers based on skill levels, favorite Garba styles, and geographical proximity.
- Enable mutual real-time messaging between matched dance partners.
- Maintain app security, prevent cross-site scripting (XSS), and prevent user ID spoofing.
- Comply with Google Play Developer Program policies and legal requirements.

---

## 4. Third-Party Service Providers & SDK Disclosures
GarbaConnect utilizes the following verified third-party SDKs to provide backend infrastructure and mobile services:
- **Google Play Services:** Core Android app support, security checks, and distribution.
- **Google Firebase Authentication:** Secure token-based session management, identity verification, and credentials handling.
- **Google Cloud Firestore:** Scalable real-time cloud database storage governed by strict server-side security rules.
- **Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics (Optional):** Aggregated, non-personally identifiable diagnostic metrics to resolve stability issues.

*We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers.*

## 5. Account & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)
Google Play requires apps that allow account creation to provide a clear, accessible path to delete accounts both inside the app and online.

### In-App Account Deletion (Instant Self-Serve):
1. Open the GarbaConnect app.
2. Tap on the **Profile** tab in the bottom navigation bar.
3. Scroll down to the account actions section.
4. Tap the **Delete Account & Data** button.
5. Confirm deletion by typing `DELETE`.
6. Your account, profile document, swipes, match connections, chat history, and Firebase Auth credentials will be permanently erased immediately.

### Web-Based Account Deletion Request:
Users may also request complete data deletion by emailing **harshalbanework@gmail.com** with the subject line *"GarbaConnect Account Deletion Request"* from their registered email address. We will verify ownership and permanently delete all records within 48 business hours.

## 6. Children's Privacy

- XSS sanitization across all dynamic UI elements.
- Secure local token storage using Android WebView sandboxing.
