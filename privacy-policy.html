Privacy Policy for GarbaConnect

Last Updated: October 18, 2026 | App Package: com.harshalbane.garbaconnect

GarbaConnect ("we", "us", "our") is designed to connect Garba dancers, enthusiasts, and Navratri celebration participants worldwide. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data collected through our Android application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password, age, gender, and city.
  • Profile & Matching Data: Dance skill level, Garba style interests, dance partner preferences, bio, avatar selection, or user-uploaded profile photo.
  • Swipes & Chat: Swipes (like, super like, pass), connections/matches, and direct in-app chat messages between mutual connections.
  • App Health & Diagnostics: Anonymous crash and performance metrics via Firebase and Google Play Services to ensure uptime and performance.

2. How We Use Data

Your information is used solely to provide core app functionality: account authentication, matchmaking between compatible dance partners, and real-time chat communication. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

3. Third-Party SDK Disclosures

  • Google Play Services: Core Android OS platform runtime, application signing verification, and in-app updates.
  • Firebase Authentication: Token-based security and credentials verification.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: Encrypted, real-time cloud data storage protected by strict server-side access rules.

4. Mandatory Account & Data Deletion Instructions

In full compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, users can permanently delete their account and associated data at any time via two mechanisms:

  1. In-App Self-Serve Deletion: Navigate to the Profile tab, scroll to the bottom, tap Delete Account & Data, and confirm deletion by typing DELETE. All profiles, photos, swipes, and messages will be permanently expunged immediately.
  2. Web Deletion Request: Send an email from your registered email address to harshalbanework@gmail.com with the subject "GarbaConnect Account Deletion". Records will be permanently deleted within 48 business hours.

5. Security

We enforce TLS 1.3 encryption for all traffic in transit, strict Firestore schema validation, XSS sanitization across client views, and sandboxed storage on the Android device.

6. Contact Developer

If you have any questions about this privacy statement, contact Harshal Bane at harshalbanework@gmail.com.
